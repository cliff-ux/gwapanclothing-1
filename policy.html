POLICY

In Gwap We Believe — Handcrafted Standards & Client Guidelines

1. Handcrafted Production Timeline

Every garment under the Gwapaffection label is custom-tailored and constructed by hand at our workshop in Nairobi, Kenya. Because we prioritize exceptional weight denim, premium panel stitching, and structured leather drapes over fast-fashion assembly lines:

  • Standard structural construction requires up to 3 business days from the moment payment confirmation is logged.
  • Highly detailed denim customizations, bespoke tailoring adjustments, or special collection sets may experience extended fulfillment tracks, which will be communicated during your WhatsApp order onboarding.

2. Secure M-PESA Payment Protocol

To eliminate checkout friction and securely manage local processing, checkout handles final parameter assembly directly via a verified manual WhatsApp business ledger framework.

  • Orders are officially initialized only after official confirmation parameters match our M-PESA transactional ledger profiles.
  • For items tagged as **Limited Drops** (e.g., Void and Ghost tracksuits), inventory holds are not guaranteed upon checkout compilation. Stock availability locks permanently only upon validation of payment.

3. Delivery & Fulfillment Logistics

Once crafting and structural quality audits finish within the 3-day production buffer, dispatch routes deploy across Nairobi and regional East African networks:

  • Nairobi Central & Local Estates: Dispatched via reliable local courier networks or custom rider services directly to your specified drop zone.
  • Max Fee (350) For deliveries Around Nairobi
  • Outside Nairobi / Regional Locations: Arranged securely via recognized regional parcel couriers or vehicle booking operators.
  • All transport fees are handled transparently and verified alongside full client data parameters inside the checkout validation process.

4. Exchange & Sizing Adjustments

As a premium handcrafted studio streetwear line, we work tirelessly to ensure your fit matches exactly. Because pieces are made to order, please adhere to our alignment guidelines:

  • We strongly advise reviewing size configurations (S, M, L, XL) and checking the model lookbook references inside our product gallery screens before finalizing checkouts.
  • Exchanges for physical size adjustments are evaluated on an item-by-item basis. Items submitted for adjustment must remain unworn, unwashed, and in pristine structural condition with original packaging intact.
