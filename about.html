THE ORIGIN STORY

Born in the vibrant, creative pulse of Nairobi, GWAPAFFECTION is where street grit meets artisan precision. We don't believe in "fast fashion." Every garment we create is a required exercise in slow, deliberate craft, manual distressing, and architectural paneling.

Every gold stitch, every rip, and every unique panel is executed by local Nairobi artisans who understand that "custom" means a required commitment to one-of-one artistry. When you wear Gwapan, you are wearing an authentic story of Kenyan identity and impeccable craft.
